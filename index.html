Questions

Do I have to accept the quotation?

No. The quotation is information. You only owe anything if you accept and sign the agreement.

Can I settle early?

Yes, at any time. Ask us for a settlement figure and we will give you the exact amount for the date you choose.

What happens if I miss a payment?

Any late fee is stated in your quotation and agreement before you sign. If you think you will struggle to pay, contact us early.

Who sees my information?

Only authorised Rolva Finance Technologies staff, and only to assess and manage your application and loan.
